Academic Project

Developed as a Semester III Web Technology project at Shah & Anchor Kutchhi Engineering College using HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript.

Our Mission

Help users plan road trips by estimating travel distance, travel time, fuel cost, destination weather, and nearby facilities through simple web technologies.

Core Stack

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Fetch API, LocalStorage, OpenRouteService, Open-Meteo API, and OpenStreetMap Overpass API.
